# Framestack
The photographers gallery.
## What is it?
The Framestack is an app developed for photographers to organize their portfolios and share their work, like an especially focused gallery. No more scrumbling your phone's gallery to search for a photo taken by your camera. 

## How it works? 
You sync your photos in the preferred device and it becomes available in all devices in wich you have the  Framestack app installed. Your photos are then organized in photographers-specific details automatically and then you can create and share albums from that specific photo travel or event.
